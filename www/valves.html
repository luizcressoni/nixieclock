When to light up on its own

On the hour and on the half hour the clock lights up on its own. At night that only wears out tubes, so both are tied to daytime. The camera is not affected: motion and faces light the clock up at any hour.

Automatic regeneration lights all six tubes at once, at the brightness set above, cycling 0 to 9. Each sweep takes 10 seconds, so 5 sweeps make about 50 seconds per session. It is prevention, not repair: no overcurrent. A session only starts with the clock dark: if somebody is in front of it at that time, it waits for the next one. If the camera sees somebody mid-session, it stops and the clock goes back to showing the time.

Cathode regeneration

Cycles the chosen tube through 0 to 9 nonstop, at full brightness, to burn off the poisoning on rarely used cathodes. Runs indefinitely, ignoring the camera, until you stop it or the clock restarts. Meanwhile the clock does not show the time and the other tubes stay dark.
